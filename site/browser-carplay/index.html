Before you connect

  1. Park safely. Enable the experimental browser bridge in DiPlay.
  2. DiPlay must allow this website origin: this page’s HTTPS origin.
  3. Enter its private IPv4 address and port below. After confirming you are parked, this page connects automatically. Allow Chrome’s local-network permission if you choose, then tap Accept in DiPlay on Android.

The external viewer’s local ws:// WebSocket link is unencrypted, including video and touch controls. Use only your own trusted private network. HTTPS protects this page, not that ws:// link.

    Check LAN reachability separately

    While parked, manually enter the HTTP health URL shown in DiPlay in the browser address bar, for example http://192.168.1.20:8765/health. A response identifying the DiPlay browser service proves that this browser can reach that APK’s health endpoint over the LAN. It does not prove the HTTPS viewer has local-network permission, that its WebSocket handshake is allowed, or that Android approved a session.

    This HTTPS page never fetches the HTTP health endpoint, because that request can be blocked as mixed content. Navigating away disconnects an active session. Return to this HTTPS viewer and confirm you are parked for a new attempt. Do not bypass browser or certificate warnings.

    Only this attempt’s stage times and numeric close code are kept in memory. No payloads, credentials, or video are logged or stored.

    Browser requirements & privacy

    Open this page over HTTPS in Chrome 147 or later with WebCodecs and Local Network Access support. Embedded WebViews, older browsers, and many in-car browsers may not support this flow. Install the latest DiPlay APK and reload this viewer together: both must support Android approval (protocol v2). A supported codec is checked when the bridge sends its configuration. H.265 support depends on the browser and device.

    If a connection is blocked, check the site’s local-network permission, the bridge’s allowed origin, and that both devices share a trusted LAN. Do not disable browser security, ignore certificate warnings, or expose the bridge to the internet.

    Use the phone’s direct connection to the car for sound. This viewer carries video and touch only; Android’s normal audio playback remains available. This viewer has no analytics, third-party scripts, internet media uploads, recording, credential storage, or microphone access. Video stays between the Android bridge and this browser. The parked checkbox is your confirmation; this page does not detect vehicle speed.
